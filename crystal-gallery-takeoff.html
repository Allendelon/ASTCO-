          Basis of Estimate & Workflow

          What the drawings support and what they don't

          • Measured from the drawings (green "DRW"): GFA by level and use (p.17), floor-to-floor heights GF 7.0 / L1 6.0 / L2 5.5 / L3 5.5 / L4 3.5 m (pp.18–24), 451 car spaces, 1,075 cinema seats, 2 substations (B1), generator + fuel tank + LPG tank (GF).
          • Derived (blue "DER"): drawing quantities combined with an engineering assumption, e.g. concrete = slab area × equivalent thickness. The assumption sits on the Assumptions tab.
          • Allowance (amber "ALW"): no drawing basis yet, e.g. piling, escalator count, external works area, site utilities.
          • The set has no structural, geotechnical, MEP, facade or specification information. Basement storey height, raft thickness and piling are assumed.

          The 3-Step Local HTML Workflow

          1. Export Schedule: export your baseline from Primavera P6 as .XER/Excel and replace ACTIVITIES in this file.
          2. Add Drawings: update AREA, HEIGHT and the perimeters when SUB3 or DD drawings with dimensions arrive.
          3. Re-price: overwrite rates in the Controls lens. Overrides show in amber and are kept in this browser.

          Ready-to-Use AI Prompt Template

          I have placed my construction schedule export (XER/Excel), the architect's area schedule and the site drawings in this folder. Please generate a single self-contained HTML file that acts as an interactive 4D/5D take-off and cost model. At the top, show a 2D section of the building that highlights work areas as they are built (brown for earthworks, grey for concrete, blue for glazing, copper for screens). At the bottom, show a synchronized Gantt schedule, an editable bill of quantities with source flags (drawing / derived / allowance), and a cumulative cashflow S-curve. Include a play/pause simulation and a Baseline vs Extension of Time (EOT) scenario with prolongation cost.

          Measurement method

          The BoQ is grouped by element, loosely following NRM1 elemental order. Quantities are net, with no waste included. Rates cover supply and installation.

          Exclusions

          Land, authority fees, design fees, financing, tenant works in Shell & Core scope, loose FF&E, escalation beyond the base date, and VAT (shown separately).
